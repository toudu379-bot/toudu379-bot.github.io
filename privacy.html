Privacy Policy

Personal Assistant · Last updated: 24 October 2026

What data the app accesses

The app accesses only my own Google account data, and only the data covered by the permissions I grant on Google's consent screen (for example Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, and Google Drive files). It accesses this data only when I ask it to.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out the requests I make. To do that, it is processed by the AI tools I use (such as Anthropic Claude) on my behalf. It is not used for advertising.

Sharing

Nothing is sold. Nothing is shared with third parties, other than the AI tools that process the data on my behalf to answer my requests.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are kept only on my own devices. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
