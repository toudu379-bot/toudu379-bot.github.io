Personal Assistant

A personal assistant app that connects my own Google account to the AI tools I use.

This app lets me, its owner, give my AI tools access to my own Google data (for example email, calendar and files) so they can help me with tasks I ask for. It acts only on my requests.

This is not a commercial service. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and no login is needed to read this page.
